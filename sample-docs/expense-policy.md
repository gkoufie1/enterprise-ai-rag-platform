# Travel and Expense Policy

Owner: Finance. Last reviewed: 2026-06-10.

## Submitting expenses
Submit expenses in the expense system within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemized receipt for every item over 25 USD. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Approval limits
- Up to 1,000 USD: approved by the employee's manager.
- 1,000 to 5,000 USD: also approved by the department director.
- Over 5,000 USD: also approved by Finance before the purchase is made.

## Travel
Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Hotel costs should not exceed 250 USD per night in most cities, or 350 USD per night in high-cost cities listed in the travel portal.

## Meals
The daily meal allowance while travelling is 75 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursed except at client events approved in advance by a director.

## Software purchases
Software and SaaS subscriptions must be requested through the IT service desk, not purchased with a corporate card, so Security can review them.
