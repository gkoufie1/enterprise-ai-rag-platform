# IT Onboarding Guide for New Employees

Owner: IT Service Desk. Last reviewed: 2026-09-20.

## Your first day
Your laptop is shipped to arrive two business days before your start date. On your first morning, sign in with the temporary password from your welcome email, then set a new password of at least 14 characters and enroll in MFA using the authenticator app. Hardware security keys are issued to engineers and administrators in the first week.

## Accounts you will receive
- Email, calendar and chat.
- Single sign-on to the HR system, expense system and travel portal.
- Engineers also receive GitHub organization access and read-only access to the development AWS account after completing security training.

## Required training
Complete security awareness training and the data handling course within your first 14 days. Access to production systems is not granted until both are complete.

## Getting help
Contact the IT Service Desk through the help portal or the #it-help chat channel. The service desk is staffed 08:00 to 18:00 local time on weekdays; for urgent security issues outside those hours, call the security hotline listed in the help portal.
