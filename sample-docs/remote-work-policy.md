# Remote Work Policy

Owner: People Operations. Last reviewed: 2026-09-01.

## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week once they have completed their 90-day introductory period. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the employee's manager and the department director, and are reviewed every twelve months.

## Equipment and security
All remote work must be done on a company-managed laptop. Personal computers, tablets and phones may not be used to access customer data. Employees must connect through the company VPN and sign in with multi-factor authentication (MFA) for every session. Sensitive customer data must never be downloaded to personal devices or personal cloud storage.

## Working from another country
Working from outside the employee's country of employment for more than 14 calendar days in a year requires prior approval from People Operations and Legal, because of tax and data-residency rules.

## Home office stipend
Remote-eligible employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 500 USD for a chair, monitor or desk, claimed through the expense system within 60 days of the arrangement starting.

## Availability
Remote employees must be reachable on chat during their team's core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 local time.
